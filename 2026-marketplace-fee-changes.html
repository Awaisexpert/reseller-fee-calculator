2026 Marketplace Fee Changes

Marketplace fees are constantly shifting. Here is a plain-English breakdown of the most recent fee updates for U.S. resellers in 2026, and what they mean for your margins.

eBay 2026 Fee Updates

eBay's standard final value fee remains at 13.6% for most categories, but they have continued to push the per-order fixed fee. Orders of $10 or less are charged $0.30, while orders over $10 are charged $0.40. Additionally, eBay continues to charge this percentage on the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping and sales tax. This means offering "free shipping" actually costs you more than just the postage.

Poshmark 2026 Fee Updates

Poshmark's fee structure remains flat and straightforward: a flat $2.95 fee for sales under $15, and a 20% commission on sales of $15 or more. The key thing to remember for 2026 is that the buyer pays the shipping, which keeps the fee base low. If you offer discounted shipping as a seller, that comes out of your pocket.

Mercari 2026 Fee Updates

Mercari currently charges a 10% selling fee, which includes payment processing. Like eBay, this 10% is applied to the item price plus any buyer-paid shipping. Be careful with Mercari's Instant Pay feature, which adds a $3 fee per transfer if you want your money immediately instead of waiting for a standard direct deposit.

Use the Mercari Calculator

Etsy 2026 Fee Updates

Etsy's fee stack consists of a 6.5% transaction fee, plus 3% + $0.25 payment processing, plus a $0.20 listing fee. However, the biggest impact on margin continues to be Offsite Ads. If your shop makes under $10,000 a year, you can opt out of Offsite Ads. If you make $10,000 or more, you are required to participate, and Etsy will take 12% (down from 15%) on sales that come from their external ads.

Use the Etsy Calculator

Depop 2026 Fee Updates

Depop currently charges no selling fee in the U.S. The only mandatory deduction is payment processing, which is 3.3% + $0.45 per transaction. However, if you use Depop's optional "Boosted Listings" feature to promote your items, they will take an additional 12% when the item sells.
