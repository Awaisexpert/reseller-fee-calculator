Etsy pricing factors

Etsy fees can affect profit in more ways than one. Sellers often track total cost at the point of sale, plus shipping and order processing.

  • Listing fees and transaction fees can add up quickly.
  • Processing fees are usually taken on the sale value.
  • Shipping charges can margin-kill if not priced correctly.

Best practice

Model your expected profit before listing so you know whether the item is worth your time and capital.
