Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay Etsy’s Offsite Ads fee?

Below $10,000 in trailing 365-day sales, eligible sellers can generally opt out. Once the shop crosses $10,000, participation is mandatory and the rate is 12% on attributed orders.

What’s Etsy’s total fee percentage?

Before fixed fees and Offsite Ads, the percentage is 9.5% (6.5% transaction plus 3% processing), plus $0.20 listing and $0.25 processing fees. Offsite Ads add 15% or 12% when applicable.

What fees does this Etsy calculator assume?

For Etsy, it assumes 9.5% (6.5% transaction plus 3% payment processing) plus $0.45 in fixed fees. These are planning estimates, so confirm current rates with the marketplace before you list.
