What should you measure to know your real profit?

Profit is not just sale price minus item cost. The true number includes shipping, fees, marketplace commissions, and tax assumptions.

  • Track item cost and shipping before selling.
  • Include marketplace fees in margin calculations.

Frequently asked questions

How is estimated profit calculated?

Buyer-paid shipping is added to the sale price, the estimated marketplace fee is subtracted, and then your shipping cost and item cost are subtracted.

Are taxes included in the profit estimate?

No. Taxes, promoted listings, refunds, and marketplace rate changes are not included, so treat the result as a planning estimate.
