Tax guide

Reseller Tax Guide for U.S. Sellers

General information about tax planning, sales tax, and profit tracking for U.S. resellers.

Tax planning is a separate issue from fee math

This guide is general information and not tax or legal advice. Product fees and tax obligations are different topics, and the rules can vary by state and business structure.

When you estimate profit, use your fee calculator first, then separate your tax assumptions from your marketplace fee assumptions. Many sellers track item cost, shipping, fees, and sale price in a spreadsheet to make sure their real margin is not overstated.

Frequently asked questions

Do resellers pay taxes on sales?

Sometimes. Tax rules vary by state and by whether you are selling as an individual, LLC, or another entity. This is a general guide only.

Should I include tax in my pricing model?

Only if you have a business-specific reason to do so. The calculator here is designed to estimate marketplace fees and profit before tax planning.
