Frequently asked questions

What fees does this Mercari calculator assume?

For Mercari, it assumes a 10% estimated fee. These are planning estimates, so confirm current rates with the marketplace before you list.

Does shipping change my profit?

Yes. Shipping you pay as the seller is subtracted from your payout. If the buyer pays shipping, enter it separately so the estimate reflects it.
