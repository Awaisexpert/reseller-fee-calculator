Compare Reseller Fees

Every marketplace takes a different cut. This table breaks down the standard seller fees for the seven most popular U.S. resale platforms so you can decide where to list your inventory.

Marketplace Standard Seller Fee Fee on Shipping? Best For
eBay 13.6% + $0.30/$0.40 Yes High-value items, broad audience
Poshmark 20% or $2.95 flat No (Buyer pays) Clothing, shoes, accessories
Mercari 10% Yes Mid-tier everyday items, electronics
Etsy 9.5% + $0.45 Yes Vintage, handmade, craft supplies
Depop 3.3% + $0.45 Yes Gen-Z fashion, streetwear, Y2K
Vinted 0% (Seller fee) No Budget clothing (Buyer pays fees)
Whatnot ~10.9% + $0.30 Yes Live selling, collectibles, hyped items

Fees are estimates based on standard U.S. rates. Always check the platform's official fee page before listing high-value items.
