How the reverse formula works

For percentage-based fees, the estimated price is calculated as (COGS + target profit + shipping + fixed fee) divided by (1 − percentage fee). This lets you price from your desired result instead of guessing.

Marketplace assumptions

  • eBay: 13.25% plus $0.30 fixed fee.
  • Poshmark: $2.95 below $15 or 20% at $15 and above.
  • Mercari and Depop: 10% estimated fee.
  • Etsy: 9.5% plus a $0.45 fixed fee.

Reverse pricing FAQs

What is a reverse fee calculator?

It starts with your costs and desired result, then estimates the sale price needed after marketplace fees.

Can I target a percentage margin?

Yes. Select listing-price margin and enter a percentage such as 30 for a 30% target margin.

Why can Poshmark have two results?

Poshmark has a flat fee below $15 and a 20% fee at $15 or more. The calculator shows any valid branch that satisfies the threshold.
