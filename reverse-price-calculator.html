Reverse pricing FAQs

How do I know what price to list at to make $50 profit?

Enter $50 for target net profit and include every out-of-pocket cost. The result is the listing price that leaves your target after the selected marketplace fee.

Does shipping affect the answer?

Yes. Shipping is treated as a seller expense. If you charge the buyer separately, verify whether your marketplace also includes that shipping charge in its fee base.

Why are two Poshmark prices sometimes shown?

Poshmark changes from a flat fee below $15 to a 20% fee at $15 and above. Near the cliff, two valid branches may reach the same target; both are displayed.
