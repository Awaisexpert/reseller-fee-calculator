Frequently asked questions

What is reverse pricing?

Reverse pricing starts with the profit you want and works backward to find the listing price needed to cover your costs and fees.

Does shipping change the price I need?

Yes. Seller-paid shipping adds to the amount you need to recover before taxes and fees.
