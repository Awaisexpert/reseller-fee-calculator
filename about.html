About

About Reseller Fee Calculator

Reseller Fee Calculator is a free set of U.S. reseller pricing tools built around one idea: work backward from the profit you want, not forward from the price you guess. Instead of asking "what do I keep if I sell at $50", you ask "what price do I need to keep $25" — and the calculator solves for you across six marketplaces.

Why this site exists

Most fee calculators only do forward math. But resellers price from the other direction: they know what they paid, they know the profit they need, and they want the listing price that makes both work.

Ours is free, runs entirely in the browser, and shows exactly what fee was used for each platform.

How we keep fees accurate

We review every marketplace's published fee schedule approximately every two months, and immediately after public rate changes. Full sources on the methodology page.

Spotted a wrong rate? Report it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your email or sell your data. The calculator runs entirely in your browser.
  • No ads. No promoted marketplace placements. Ranking is not for sale.
  • No tax, legal, or financial advice.
