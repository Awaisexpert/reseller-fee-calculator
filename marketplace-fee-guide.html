Which fees cut into a reseller’s margin?

Success in resale is not just about selling at a higher price. The true profit depends on what remains after fees and shipping.

  • Marketplace commissions reduce the sale price.
  • Buyer shipping costs affect how much you keep.
  • Payment processing and taxes can quietly lower net profit.
