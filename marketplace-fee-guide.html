Marketplace Fee Guide for Resellers

A plain-English guide to the main fees that cut into your margin when reselling in the U.S.

What matters most

Marketplace fees are not the only cost that matters. Your actual profit depends on the sale price, item cost, shipping, refund risk, and category-specific fees.

Frequently asked questions

Which fee matters most?

For most items, the biggest variable is your total fee percentage. Shipping and category-specific charges can matter just as much if you are pricing close to the margin.

Do I need to include tax in my estimate?

No. Taxes are not included in these estimates. This site is a planning tool and not tax advice.
