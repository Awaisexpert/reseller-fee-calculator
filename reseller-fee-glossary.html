Reseller Fee Glossary

Every fee term a reseller runs into, defined plainly. A final value fee is the percentage a marketplace charges on the sale amount. COGS is what you paid for the item. Net payout is what the marketplace deposits. Net profit is what you actually keep after all costs. The terms below cover how these work across eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy, Depop, and Vinted.

Marketplace fee terms

Final value fee

The percentage a marketplace charges on the total sale amount, usually including buyer-paid shipping. eBay uses this term; other platforms call it a selling fee or commission. On eBay, the rate is typically 13.6% for most categories.

Selling fee / commission

The general term for a marketplace's percentage cut. Poshmark charges 20% on sales of $15 or more, Mercari charges 10%, and Etsy charges 6.5% transaction fee plus 3% payment processing.

Payment processing fee

A charge for moving money from the buyer to the seller, usually collected by the marketplace's payment partner. Etsy charges 3% + $0.25, Depop charges 3.3% + $0.45, and many platforms bundle processing into the selling fee.

Per-order fixed fee

A flat dollar amount charged per sale. eBay charges $0.30 on orders of $10 or less and $0.40 above. Etsy charges $0.20 per listing plus a $0.25 payment processing fixed fee.

Buyer protection fee

A fee some marketplaces charge buyers to fund purchase protection. On Poshmark and Vinted the buyer pays this, not the seller, so it does not reduce seller payout. On eBay, buyer protection is included in the final value fee.

Pricing and profit terms

COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)

What you paid to acquire the item, plus any inbound shipping or refurbishment to prepare it for resale. COGS is the starting point for every profit calculation.

Sale price

The item price the buyer sees on the listing. Does not include shipping or tax unless the marketplace bundles them.

Net payout

The amount the marketplace deposits into your account after subtracting its fees. Net payout = sale price + buyer-paid shipping − platform fees.

Net profit

Net payout minus COGS and seller-paid shipping. This is what you actually keep. A common mistake is to treat net payout as profit — it is not, because your item cost has not been subtracted yet.

Profit margin

Net profit divided by sale price, expressed as a percentage. A 20% margin on a $100 sale means $20 in net profit. Margins help compare items at different price points.

ROI (Return on Investment)

Net profit divided by COGS, expressed as a percentage. A $20 profit on a $10 item is 200% ROI. ROI measures efficiency of capital; margin measures efficiency of revenue. Both matter depending on your sourcing strategy.

Break-even price

The listing price at which net profit equals zero. Below this price you lose money on the sale after fees and costs. Knowing your break-even price helps when negotiating offers.

Listing price

The price you set on the marketplace. It may or may not equal the price the buyer actually pays after shipping, tax, and offers.

Target profit

The dollar profit you want to make on a sale. The target profit calculator works backward from this number to find the listing price required.

Target margin

The percentage margin you want to make on a sale, rather than a fixed dollar amount. Targeting 30% margin on a $50 sale means aiming for $15 profit.

Shipping terms

Buyer-paid shipping

The shipping amount the buyer pays at checkout. Some marketplaces include this in the fee base (eBay, Mercari, Etsy); others do not (Poshmark). Always check which model the marketplace uses.

Seller-paid shipping

Shipping you cover yourself. Subtracted from your payout like any other expense. On Poshmark, sellers typically offer "free shipping" discounts that cut into profit.

Advertising and promotion terms

Promoted listing

An optional eBay advertising feature that boosts a listing's visibility for a seller-chosen ad rate. The promoted percentage is charged on top of the final value fee when a promoted listing results in a sale.

Offsite Ads

An Etsy advertising program that charges the seller 15% or 12% of the order total when Etsy advertises a listing on external sites and a shopper buys through that ad. Below $10,000 in trailing 12-month sales, sellers can opt out; above it, participation is required.

Boosted listing

Depop's paid promotion option. Costs 12% of the item price when a boosted listing sells, charged on top of standard payment processing.

Instant Pay

Mercari's optional faster-payout feature, which costs $3 per transfer. Not required; standard direct deposit is free.

Calculator terms

Target profit calculator

A calculator that works backward from a desired dollar profit to find the minimum listing price after fees, shipping, and costs. Also called a reverse price calculator.

Reverse price calculator

A calculator that starts with costs and a desired profit, then solves for the listing price required after percentage and fixed fees. Useful for sourcing decisions before you buy inventory.

Forward profit calculator

A calculator that starts with a known sale price and computes the resulting profit. Useful for verifying a price you have already set or for comparing platforms on the same item.
