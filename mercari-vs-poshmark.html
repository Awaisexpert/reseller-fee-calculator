Frequently asked questions

What fees does this Mercari and Poshmark calculator assume?

For Mercari, it assumes a 10% estimated fee. For Poshmark, it assumes $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% at $15 and above. These are planning estimates, so confirm current rates with the marketplace before you list.

Does shipping change my profit?

Yes. Shipping you pay as the seller is subtracted from your payout. If the buyer pays shipping, enter it separately so the estimate reflects it.
