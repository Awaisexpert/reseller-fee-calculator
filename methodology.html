Transparency

Methodology

Every calculator on this site uses published seller fee rates, verified against official marketplace pages. We include final value fees, payment processing, and platform-specific rules. We exclude taxes, promoted listings, refunds, and most category-specific rate variations unless noted on the specific page.

What we include

  • Final value fee or selling commission — the percentage a marketplace charges on the sale amount.
  • Payment processing fees where charged separately (Etsy, Depop).
  • Per-order fixed fees (eBay $0.30/$0.40, Etsy $0.20/$0.25).
  • Platform thresholds like Poshmark's $2.95 flat fee under $15.
  • Buyer-paid shipping in fee base where applicable (eBay, Mercari, Depop, Etsy).
  • Seller-paid shipping as an expense.

What we exclude

  • Sales tax collected by the marketplace.
  • Promoted listings, Offsite Ads, Boosted Listings.
  • Refunds, returns, cancellations.
  • Category-specific rates. eBay and Etsy vary by category.
  • Instant Pay or fast payout fees.
  • Subscriptions (eBay Store, Etsy Plus).

Reverse calculation formula

Listing price = (COGS + target profit + seller-paid shipping + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fee)

For Poshmark, the calculator tests both the flat $2.95 and 20% branches and returns any valid result.
