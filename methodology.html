Methodology

This page documents exactly how Reseller Fee Calculator produces its estimates. Every calculator on this site uses published seller fee rates, verified against official marketplace pages. We include final value fees, payment processing, and platform-specific rules. We exclude taxes, promoted listings, refunds, and most category-specific rate variations unless noted on the specific page.

What we include in each estimate

  • Final value fee or selling commission — the percentage a marketplace charges on the sale amount.
  • Payment processing fees where the marketplace charges them separately (Etsy, Depop, Whatnot).
  • Per-order fixed fees where applicable (eBay's $0.30 or $0.40, Etsy's $0.20 listing and $0.25 processing).
  • Platform-specific thresholds such as Poshmark's $2.95 flat fee under $15.
  • Buyer-paid shipping in the fee base for platforms that include it (eBay, Mercari, Depop, Etsy).
  • Seller-paid shipping as an expense subtracted from payout.

What we exclude

  • Sales tax collected by the marketplace on the buyer's behalf. Some platforms charge fees on tax; we do not model it.
  • Promoted listings, promoted listings percentage, ads (eBay Promoted Listings, Etsy Offsite Ads, Depop Boosted Listings).
  • Refunds, returns, and cancellations.
  • Category-specific rates. eBay and Etsy vary by category. We use the most common rate and note it.
  • Instant Pay or fast payout fees (Mercari Instant Pay $3, for example).
  • Subscription fees (eBay Store, Etsy Plus).

How the reverse calculation works

Our reverse calculator works backward from your target profit. For a percentage-based fee, the formula is:

Listing price = (COGS + target profit + seller-paid shipping + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fee)

For Poshmark, which has a $15 threshold, the calculator tests both branches (flat $2.95 and 20%) and returns any valid result. For a percentage-of-price target instead of a dollar target, the formula adds the margin to the denominator.

Verification schedule

We re-check every marketplace rate approximately every two months, and immediately after any public rate change announcement. The most recent verification for this site is November 9, 2026.

Limits of these estimates

These are planning estimates only, not financial, tax, or accounting advice. Your actual payout may differ based on your account, category, buyer location, tax jurisdiction, and any promotional services you use. Always verify current rates with the marketplace directly before you list.
