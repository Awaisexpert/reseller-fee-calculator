Methodology

How the calculations work

The site uses a simple model that starts with your item cost, shipping, and target profit or sale price.

Formula structure

The reverse profit calculator solves the price needed to hit your target using the equation: price = (cost + shipping + target + fixed fee) / (1 - rate).

For margin mode, the formula is price = (cost + shipping + fixed fee) / (1 - rate - margin).
