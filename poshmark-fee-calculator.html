How do Poshmark fees work?

Poshmark fees can be easier to estimate when you know the sale price and whether the buyer pays shipping or the seller covers it.

  • Marketplace commission is usually a percentage of sale price.
  • Shipping costs may be separate from the item sale fee.
  • Net payout helps estimate the item’s real profitability.

Frequently asked questions

What fees does this Poshmark calculator assume?

For Poshmark, it assumes $2.95 on sales under $15 and 20% at $15 and above. These are planning estimates, so confirm current rates with the marketplace before you list.

Does shipping change my profit?

Yes. Shipping you pay as the seller is subtracted from your payout. If the buyer pays shipping, enter it separately so the estimate reflects it.
