How Poshmark fees work

Poshmark fees can be easier to estimate when you know the sale price and whether the buyer pays shipping or the seller covers it.

  • Marketplace commission is usually a percentage of sale price.
  • Shipping costs may be separate from the item sale fee.
  • Net payout helps estimate the item’s real profitability.
