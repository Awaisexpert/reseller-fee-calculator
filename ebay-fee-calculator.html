What does eBay charge U.S. sellers?

For many U.S. sales, eBay charges a final value fee on the total sale amount. Additional costs can include shipping adjustments, payment processing, and taxes depending on the item.

  • Final value fee based on sale price.
  • Payment processing charge usually applied to the transaction.
  • Shipping can affect the net payout if you cover postage.

Frequently asked questions

What fees does this eBay calculator assume?

For eBay, it assumes 13.6% of the total sale amount (item price plus buyer-paid shipping) for most categories, plus $0.30 per order of $10 or less and $0.40 per order over $10. The percentage also applies to sales tax collected from the buyer, which this calculator does not include. Some categories have different rates. These are planning estimates, so confirm current rates with the marketplace before you list.

Does shipping change my profit?

Yes. Shipping you pay as the seller is subtracted from your payout. If the buyer pays shipping, enter it separately so the estimate reflects it.
