Vinted Fee Calculator — Sellers Keep 100%

For Vinted sales in the US, sellers pay 0% commission. Buyers pay their own Buyer Protection fee instead, so the item price and seller-paid shipping flow through to your payout.

How Vinted seller fees work

Vinted’s US model puts the Buyer Protection fee on the buyer rather than charging the seller a selling commission. This calculator therefore shows the full item price, less any shipping amount you personally pay. Optional seller services or adjustments are not included.
