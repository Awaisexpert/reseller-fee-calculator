Vinted Fee Calculator — Sellers Keep 100%

For Vinted sales in the US, sellers pay 0% commission. Buyers pay their own Buyer Protection fee instead, so the item price and seller-paid shipping flow through to your payout.

How do Vinted seller fees work?

Vinted’s US model puts the Buyer Protection fee on the buyer rather than charging the seller a selling commission. This calculator therefore shows the full item price, less any shipping amount you personally pay. Optional seller services or adjustments are not included.

How do you price an item to hit a profit target on Vinted?

With no seller fee, the listing price is simply item cost + shipping you pay + target profit. A $10 item with $5 seller-paid shipping and a $25 profit target needs a $40.00 listing. To compare the price you would need on other marketplaces, use the reverse price calculator, or read the marketplace fee guide.

Frequently asked questions

Does Vinted charge sellers a fee in the U.S.?

For Vinted sales in the US, sellers pay 0% commission. Buyers pay their own Buyer Protection fee instead.

What is not included in this Vinted estimate?

Optional seller services or adjustments are not included.

How much will I receive if I sell a $50 item on Vinted?

With no seller fee, you receive $50.00 if the buyer pays shipping, or $45.00 if you pay $5 for shipping.

Is Vinted cheaper than eBay or Poshmark for sellers?

On a $50 sale with $5 seller-paid shipping, this site estimates you keep $45.00 on Vinted, $37.80 on eBay and $35.00 on Poshmark, using the fee assumptions on each calculator page. Fees are only one factor: audience, how fast items sell, and the price buyers will pay also differ by marketplace.

Who pays Buyer Protection on Vinted?

In Vinted’s U.S. model, the buyer pays Buyer Protection, not the seller. See Vinted’s official help page for current details.

How do I price an item to hit a profit target on Vinted?

Because there is no seller fee, list at item cost plus the shipping you pay plus your target profit. For example, $10 cost + $5 shipping + $25 profit = $40.00.
