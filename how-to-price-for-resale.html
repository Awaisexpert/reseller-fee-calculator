Pricing guide

How to Price Items for Resale

The resale pricing formula is: listing price equals COGS plus shipping plus target profit plus fixed fees, all divided by one minus the percentage fee. In plain English: add your costs and target profit, then gross up so the marketplace's percentage cut is covered. Use the target profit calculator to solve it in seconds.

The 5-step resale pricing workflow

Step 1 — Know your COGS

What you paid, plus inbound shipping, plus cleaning/repair. A $5 item with $3 inbound shipping is really an $8 item.

Step 2 — Estimate platform fees

Multiply expected price by platform percentage, add fixed per-order fee. eBay 13.6% + $0.30/$0.40. Poshmark 20% or $2.95. Etsy 9.5% + $0.45.

Step 3 — Account for shipping

Buyer-paid shipping is in the fee base on eBay, Mercari, Etsy. Seller-paid shipping is always an expense. Free shipping bundles into price, raising fees.

Step 4 — Set a target profit

Full-time resellers target 30% margin on high turnover, 50%+ on slow movers. Start with $10-$20 per item if unsure.

Step 5 — Solve for listing price

(COGS + shipping + target + fixed) ÷ (1 − percentage). Or use the target profit calculator across all six platforms.

Common pricing mistakes

  • Pricing from COGS × 2 or × 3. Ignores fees. $10 × 3 = $30, but after 13.6% + $0.40 you keep $25.52.
  • Forgetting buyer shipping is in fee base. eBay charges 13.6% on the shipping the buyer pays too.
  • Treating net payout as profit. Profit subtracts COGS and shipping.
  • Ignoring Poshmark's $15 threshold. $2.95 flat under $15 changes optimal pricing.
  • Not modeling promoted listings or Offsite Ads. Add 8%–15% when triggered.
  • Pricing to round numbers. A $50 nets $37.80 profit; a $49 nets $36.76. The $49 may sell better.

How pricing differs by marketplace

  • eBay: 13.6% + $0.40, includes buyer shipping and tax in fee base. Best for higher-priced items.
  • Poshmark: 20% above $15, $2.95 below. Buyer pays shipping. Best for fashion.
  • Mercari: 10% with buyer shipping. Best for mid-priced everyday goods.
  • Etsy: 9.5% + $0.45 plus Offsite Ads. Best for handmade/vintage.
  • Depop: 3.3% + $0.45 processing only. Best for fashion.
  • Vinted: 0% seller fee. Smaller U.S. audience.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best markup for reselling?

Most resellers target 2x-3x COGS, but a flat multiplier ignores fees. Target 20%-40% margin on sale price and solve for listing price.

How do I calculate resale price from target profit?

(COGS + shipping + target + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fee). With $10 COGS, $5 shipping, $25 target, 13.6% + $0.40, price is ~$46.76.

Should I include shipping in my pricing?

Yes. Buyer-paid shipping is in the fee base on eBay, Mercari, Etsy. Seller-paid shipping is an expense.

What is a good profit margin for resellers?

25%-40% margin after fees. Slow movers tolerate higher margins; high turnover works at 15%-25%.

Do I need to pay taxes on resale profit?

Yes, generally taxable income in the U.S. See the reseller tax guide. This is not tax advice.
