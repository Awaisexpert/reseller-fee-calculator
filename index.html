US E-Commerce Marketplace Fee Breakdown (2026)

Accurately calculating seller fees before listing inventory is critical to running a profitable reselling business. This multi-platform tool computes expected costs across top US retail platforms in real time.

eBay Selling Fees

Most categories on eBay incur a standard Final Value Fee of approximately 13.25% to 13.6% plus a $0.30 to $0.40 per-order fixed processing charge. Fees are calculated against the total transaction amount paid by the buyer, including shipping costs and applicable sales tax.

Poshmark Selling Fees

Poshmark uses a straightforward fee structure: a flat commission of $2.95 for items priced under $15, and a flat 20% commission for items sold at $15 or higher.

Mercari Selling Fees

Mercari evaluates seller commission directly based on gross sale pricing. Standard estimated platform fee structures calculate at roughly 10% of total revenue.

Etsy Selling & Processing Fees

Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee per unit alongside a 6.5% transaction fee. Additional payment processing fees (typically 3% + $0.25) apply based on standard US merchant processing schedules.
