Frequently asked questions

How does the target profit calculator work?

It works backward from your target. It adds item cost, target profit, seller-paid shipping, and fixed fees, then removes the percentage fee from the equation.

Which marketplaces does the calculator compare?

eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy, Depop, and Vinted.

Can I target a profit margin instead of a dollar amount?

Yes. Choose the margin option and enter a target such as 30 for a 30% margin on the listing price.
