US E-Commerce Marketplace Fee Breakdown

Accurately calculating seller fees before purchasing inventory is essential for running a sustainable reselling business. This multi-platform tool computes expected costs across top US retail platforms in real time.

Marketplace Commission Rates

  • eBay: Standard Final Value Fees range from 13.25% to 13.6% plus a $0.30 to $0.40 per-order transaction charge.
  • Poshmark: Flat fee of $2.95 for sales under $15, or a straight 20% commission for items sold at $15 or higher.
  • Mercari: Standard platform fee structures average roughly 10% on gross item totals.
  • Etsy: 6.5% transaction fee plus a $0.20 listing fee and 3% + $0.25 standard payment processing rates.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate net profit when flipping items?

Net profit is calculated as: (Selling Price + Buyer Paid Shipping) - Platform Fees - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) - Seller Paid Shipping.

Why does Poshmark take a flat 20% commission?

Poshmark covers buyer/seller protection and simplifies shipping labels through USPS Priority Flat Rate, absorbing operational logistics into their 20% flat fee structure.

Which platform has the lowest selling fees for cheap items?

For items priced under $15, platforms charging percentage-based rates (like eBay or Mercari) often yield higher net margins than Poshmark's $2.95 minimum charge.
