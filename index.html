Frequently asked questions

How does the target profit calculator work?

It works backward from your target. It adds item cost, target profit, seller-paid shipping, and fixed fees, then removes the percentage fee from the equation.

Which marketplaces does the calculator compare?

eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Etsy, and Depop. Vinted has its own Vinted fee calculator.

Can I target a profit margin instead of a dollar amount?

Yes. Choose the percentage option and enter a target such as 30 for a 30% margin on the listing price.

What is not included in these estimates?

These are planning estimates. Optional promoted listings, refunds, taxes, category charges, and marketplace rate changes are not included.
